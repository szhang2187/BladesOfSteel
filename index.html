Help

Player 1: A and D move, W jumps, W again double jumps, E attacks, and R dashes.

Player 2: Left and Right arrows move, Up jumps, Up again double jumps, Enter attacks, and / dashes.

Weapons: You start with fists (8 damage). Swords (20 damage, longer reach) appear on glowing platforms. Stand at one and press your attack key to grab it.

Dashing briefly makes you invulnerable.

Controls: A and D move, Space jumps (twice to double jump), left click attacks, right click throws your sword, E grabs a sword, and Q dashes.

Weapons: You start with fists (8 damage). Swords (20 damage, longer reach) appear on glowing platforms. Stand at one and press E to grab it.

Throw: A thrown sword arcs down and deals 50 damage. A miss sticks where it lands, for anyone to grab.

Dash: You can't be hit while dashing, so use Q to slip through an attack.
